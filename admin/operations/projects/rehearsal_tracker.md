## Rehearsal Block Tracker (bonus mini-template)

> **Template.** Fill in a fresh copy per rehearsal block. The blanks here are by design —
> unlike the governing documents, this is meant to be filled in by hand.
**Studio:** __________________  **Block dates:** __________ to __________  
- Recurring day/time: __________________  Rate: $______/hr  Hours: ______  
- Total expected cost: $________  Cancellation policy: ____________________  
- Notes (heels/chairs/filming rules): ____________________________________

The rest of this document is the information to capture for any booking, drawn
from the **Venue Deal Sheet** and the **Rehearsal Block Tracker** above. It is
deliberately platform-neutral: an earlier draft framed it as a WildApricot
configuration, but WildApricot was never set up. Use it as a checklist —
in Drive, in a doc, or wherever the event is being planned.

The **Approvals** section below is the part that is not optional: those fields
exist to evidence the Co-Director approvals that Articles 11 and 19 require
before CAN.dy is committed to anything.

## Event types

- **Rehearsal Block (Studio Rental)**
- **Performance (Theater/Bar)**
- **Video Shoot**

## Information to capture

**Core**
- Venue Name (text)
- Venue Address (text)
- Venue Contact Name (text)
- Venue Contact Email/Phone (text)
- CAN.dy Lead Co-Director (dropdown/text)
- Day-of Contact (text)

**Schedule**
- Load-in Time (time)
- Start Time (time)
- End Time (time)
- Strike/Exit Time (time)
- Notes on Access/Parking (text area)

**Rules / Technical**
- Capacity / Occupancy (number)
- Heels Allowed? (yes/no)
- Chair Use Allowed? (yes/no)
- Sound Available? (yes/no)
- Sound Input (dropdown: Aux/Bluetooth/XLR/Other)
- Recording Permitted? (yes/no)
- Recording Permission Link (URL) *(paste email/pdf link in Drive)*

**Money**
- Base Fee (currency)
- Deposit Amount (currency)
- Deposit Due Date (date)
- Additional Fees (currency)
- Additional Fees Detail (text area)
- Cancellation/Reschedule Terms (text area)
- Payment Method (dropdown: card/ACH/check/other)
- Payment Deadline (date)

**Insurance / Contract**
- Insurance Required? (yes/no)
- Insurance Amount Required (currency/text)
- Additional Insured Required? (yes/no)
- Contract Link (URL) *(Drive link)*
- Contracting Party Listed Correctly? (yes/no)

**Approvals (bylaws compliance)**
- Co-Director Approval #1 (yes/no)
- Co-Director Approval #2 (yes/no)
- Other Co-Director Reviewed (yes/no)
- Treasurer Notified of Deadlines (yes/no)
- Secretary Saved Docs (yes/no)

**Ticketing / Revenue (performance only)**
- Tickets Sold By (dropdown: CAN.dy/Venue/Other)
- Ticketing Link (URL)
- Door Split / Guarantee / Bar Minimum (text area)
- Merch Allowed? (yes/no)
- Age Policy Notes (text area)

**Budget sanity**
- Expected Income (currency)
- Expected Expenses (currency)
- Deficit Approved in Writing? (yes/no)

## Attachments / links

Keep documents in Drive rather than attached to any one tool, and record the
links against the event:
  - **Contract Link**
  - **Recording Permission Link**
  - (optional) **Invoice/Quote Link**
